# IELTS Writing Task 2: 8 Steps to Success
#####  _Do you need to improve your Writing Task 2 score but don’t know where to start_?
This article will give you a free slideshow to guide you through our 8 steps to Task 2 Success.
Over the past 11 years, our team has read, corrected and returned over 10,000+ IELTS essays. This experience has given us an elaborate insight into the key features that make up a high-scoring Task 2 essay – the bulk of which you can find on our [Writing Academy](https://www.ieltsadvantage.com/join-our-vip-academy/) online course.
But we know that our course isn’t an option for everyone, so we created this strategic slideshow for you. Inside it, we condense the key lessons that we’ve learned from correcting **10,000+ IELTS essays** into 8 steps to Writing Task 2 success:
The goal of this presentation is to show you how Task 2 success isn’t the result of good luck, ability or examiners’ bias. Writing Task 2 success is simply a case of taking the steps required to give the examiner what they need.
